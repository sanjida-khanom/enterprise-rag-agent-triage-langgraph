# Employee Leave Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-004 | Effective: 1 January 2026 | Owner: People & Organization

## 1. Annual Leave
Confirmed full-time employees are entitled to 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 1.67 days per completed month of service.

A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the following calendar year. Days carried forward must be consumed by 31 March, after which they lapse without encashment.

Employees in their probation period accrue annual leave but may not consume it until confirmation.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 14 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward.

Absence exceeding two consecutive working days requires a registered medical practitioner's certificate submitted through the HR portal within five working days of return.

## 3. Parental Leave
Maternity leave is 180 calendar days at full pay, available for the first two surviving children. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 60 days of the birth.

## 4. Application Procedure
All leave must be applied for through the HR self-service portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for sick and emergency leave, which must be reported to the line manager by 10:00 AM on the first day of absence.

Approval authority rests with the direct line manager. Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days additionally requires divisional head approval.

## 5. Encashment
Unused annual leave is encashed only upon separation from the company, at basic salary rate, capped at 20 days.
