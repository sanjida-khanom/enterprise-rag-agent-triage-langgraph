# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy
Document ID: FIN-POL-011 | Effective: 1 April 2026 | Owner: Finance

## 1. Scope
Applies to all employees incurring expenses on company business.

## 2. Claim Deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days but within 60 days require divisional head approval. Claims older than 60 days will not be reimbursed.

## 3. Domestic Travel Limits
Daily meal allowance inside Dhaka is BDT 800. Outside Dhaka the daily meal allowance is BDT 1,200.

Hotel accommodation is capped at BDT 6,000 per night for Grade 1 to Grade 3 employees and BDT 9,000 per night for Grade 4 and above.

Intercity travel should use air-conditioned bus or train as standard. Air travel requires prior divisional head approval and is permitted where the one-way journey exceeds six hours by road.

## 4. Local Conveyance
Ride-sharing and taxi fares are reimbursable with a digital receipt. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at BDT 25 per kilometre.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcoholic beverages, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and expenses for accompanying family members are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## 6. Approval Matrix
Claims up to BDT 20,000: line manager. BDT 20,001 to BDT 100,000: divisional head. Above BDT 100,000: Chief Financial Officer.
