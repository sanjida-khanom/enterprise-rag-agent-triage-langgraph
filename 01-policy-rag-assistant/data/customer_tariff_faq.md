# Customer Care Reference: Tariff and Package FAQ
Document ID: CX-REF-009 | Effective: 1 June 2026 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. Package Migration
Prepaid subscribers may change their package once per 30-day cycle at no charge. A second change within the same cycle carries a BDT 30 migration fee.

Postpaid subscribers may change their plan only at the start of a billing cycle. Mid-cycle plan changes are not permitted; the request is queued and applied on the next bill date.

## 2. Balance and Validity
Prepaid main account balance does not expire while the SIM remains active. A SIM is deactivated after 90 consecutive days with no revenue-generating activity, and permanently recycled after a further 90 days.

Data pack validity is counted from the moment of activation, not from midnight.

## 3. Emergency Balance
Eligible prepaid subscribers may request emergency balance up to BDT 100. The amount plus a service charge is deducted from the next recharge. Eligibility requires at least 90 days of active usage history.

## 4. Number Portability
Mobile Number Portability requests require a valid national ID matching the SIM registration, all outstanding dues cleared, and the connection to have been active with the current operator for at least 90 days. Porting completes within 3 working days.

## 5. Bill Disputes
Postpaid billing disputes must be raised within 30 days of bill generation. During investigation the disputed amount is not treated as overdue, but undisputed amounts remain payable by the due date.

## 6. SIM Replacement
SIM replacement requires the registered owner to attend in person with the original national ID. The replacement fee is BDT 150. Biometric re-verification is mandatory.
